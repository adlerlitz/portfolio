Honeycomb: The World Beyond

Quest design, dialogue, worldbuilding and voice direction for Frozen Way’s open-world survival game.

Quests that teach through play

I designed quests that introduce the player to core mechanics while remaining embedded in the narrative. I tested and iterated on the quests myself to make sure the gameplay integration held up in practice.

Voice direction

I directed two voice actresses asynchronously, preparing recording scripts and detailed direction notes.

The story trailer

I created the initial outline, reviewed the storyboard, and wrote the voice-over dialogue and recording instructions. I also briefed animators on emotional cues and scene-specific requirements, then provided feedback during animation.
