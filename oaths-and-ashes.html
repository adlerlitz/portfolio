Oaths & Ashes

Editing, additional writing and narrative QA for Revive The Spark’s Vampire: The Masquerade visual novel.

I worked on developmental and stylistic editing, wrote additional scenes, and playtested the visual novel. The work brought together close attention to prose and dialogue with the practical demands of a branching game.

My commission covered editing and narrative QA: reading how scenes worked on the page, then checking how they played.
