House Flipper Remastered Collection

Adapting email-based quests into voiced scenes, with character briefs for artists and actors.

A remaster brings an existing audience and their memories of the original game. I reimagined the email-based quests as fully voiced cutscenes, preserving the charm and personality of the original text while expanding it into scenes worth following.

I prepared briefs for the art team and voice actors, helping character, tone and pacing translate from the page to performance.
