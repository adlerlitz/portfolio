Hairdresser Simulator

27 characters, each returning to the salon three times over the course of a personal story.

I designed every one of these characters, building their arcs around the intimate (and sometimes ridiculous) moments that can happen in a salon chair.

With each visit, players uncover an event or shift in the character’s life that brought them back, whether for comfort, reinvention or a bit of peace. Across three visits, those glimpses let the player people-watch and piece together who they are.

The result is the fantasy of a hairdresser who becomes a trusted confidante and a witness to everyday chaos.
